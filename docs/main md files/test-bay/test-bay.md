## Module 1 — History of Gas Turbine Engines

1. Which of the following best describes the principle of jet propulsion?
	- **a.** The conversion of fuel into mechanical energy by a reciprocating motion
	- **b.** The force is generated in the same direction as the exhaust gases
	- **c.** The force is produced opposite to the discharge of fluid under pressure
	- **d.** The suction of air to create a vacuum thrust
2. Jet propulsion operates according to which of Newton’s Laws of Motion?
	- **a.** First Law
	- **b.** Second Law
	- **c.** Third Law
	- **d.** Fourth Law
3. Who devised the aeolipile, an early steam-powered toy demonstrating jet propulsion?
	- **a.** Isaac Newton
	- **b.** John Barber
	- **c.** Hero of Alexandria
	- **d.** Frank Whittle
4. Who was granted the first patent covering a gas turbine in 1791?
	- **a.** Sanford Moss
	- **b.** John Barber
	- **c.** Hans von Ohain
	- **d.** Frank Whittle
5. What was Dr. Sanford Moss known for in the early 1900s?
	- **a.** Building the first supersonic jet
	- **b.** Developing the first successful turbojet
	- **c.** Creating the turbo-supercharger driven by exhaust gases
	- **d.** Designing the first commercial jetliner
6. Frank Whittle developed the first successful turbojet engine and received his patent in what year?
	- **a.** 1925
	- **b.** 1930
	- **c.** 1936
	- **d.** 1941
7. The Heinkel He-178, recognized as the first jet-propelled aircraft, was designed by:
	- **a.** Frank Whittle
	- **b.** Hans von Ohain
	- **c.** John Barber
	- **d.** Sanford Moss
8. Which type of jet engine has no moving parts and relies on forward motion to compress incoming air?
	- **a.** Pulse Jet
	- **b.** Ramjet
	- **c.** Scramjet
	- **d.** Turbojet
9. In a scramjet engine, combustion occurs in:
	- **a.** Subsonic airflow
	- **b.** Supersonic airflow
	- **c.** Static airflow
	- **d.** Rotating airflow
10. Which of the following jet engines does not rely on atmospheric air for propulsion?
	 - **a.** Gas turbine engine
	 - **b.** Ramjet
	 - **c.** Scramjet
	 - **d.** Rocket engine

## Module 2 — Types of Gas Turbine Engines

1. Which of the following best describes a turbojet engine?
	- **a.** Converts almost all energy into shaft horsepower
	- **b.** Drives a propeller through reduction gears
	- **c.** Produces thrust by accelerating exhaust gases through a nozzle
	- **d.** Uses a free power turbine to drive a shaft
2. The engine pressure ratio (EPR) in a turbojet engine is the ratio between:
	- **a.** Compressor inlet temperature and turbine inlet temperature
	- **b.** Turbine discharge pressure and inlet air pressure
	- **c.** Compressor discharge pressure and turbine inlet pressure
	- **d.** Exhaust pressure and compressor discharge pressure
3. What is a “spool” in a turbojet engine?
	- **a.** A device that measures engine performance
	- **b.** A turbine and compressor mounted on the same shaft
	- **c.** The propeller gearbox unit
	- **d.** The exhaust nozzle assembly
4. In a turboprop engine, the main power output is delivered to:
	- **a.** The jet nozzle
	- **b.** The propeller via a reduction gear system
	- **c.** The turbine only
	- **d.** The free power turbine
5. Why is reduction gearing necessary in turboprop engines?
	- **a.** To increase thrust by accelerating exhaust gases
	- **b.** To allow the propeller to operate at slower, more efficient speeds
	- **c.** To improve fuel injection timing
	- **d.** To control air intake pressure
6. The total output of a turboprop engine, combining shaft horsepower and residual jet thrust, is called:
	- **a.** Brake horsepower
	- **b.** Total engine power (TEP)
	- **c.** Equivalent Shaft Horsepower (ESHP)
	- **d.** Effective Thrust Power (ETP)
7. A turboshaft engine differs from a turboprop mainly because it:
	- **a.** Produces thrust through a nozzle
	- **b.** Drives a helicopter rotor or other mechanical load via a shaft
	- **c.** Has no turbine stages
	- **d.** Uses a ducted fan instead of a propeller
8. In a turboshaft engine, the free power turbine:
	- **a.** It is mechanically connected to the compressor
	- **b.** Converts remaining gas energy into mechanical shaft power
	- **c.** Increases air pressure before combustion
	- **d.** It is used only during startup
9. The main difference between turboprop and turboshaft engines is that:
	- **a.** Turboshaft engines produce more exhaust thrust
	- **b.** Turboprop engines drive helicopter rotors
	- **c.** Turboprops may produce some thrust from exhaust, while turboshafts primarily produce shaft power
	- **d.** Turboshafts are designed for supersonic flight
10. Ducted and unducted propfan engines are advanced designs that:
	 - **a.** Operate at subsonic speeds only
	 - **b.** Use composite materials for lighter, faster blades
	 - **c.** Have lower fuel efficiency than turbofans
	 - **d.** Cannot exceed Mach 0.7

## Module 3 — Sections of a Gas Turbine Engine

1. Which of the following components belong to the cold section of a gas turbine engine?
	- **a.** Turbine and Exhaust
	- **b.** Air Inlet and Compressor
	- **c.** Combustion and Turbine
	- **d.** Compressor and Exhaust
2. The main function of the air inlet is to:
	- **a.** Increase the speed of exhaust gases
	- **b.** Convert pressure energy into velocity energy
	- **c.** Recover total pressure and deliver uniform airflow to the compressor
	- **d.** Supply fuel to the combustion chamber
3. Which type of air inlet is specifically designed for stationary or slow-moving aircraft such as helicopters or test stands?
	- **a.** Supersonic inlet
	- **b.** Subsonic inlet
	- **c.** Bellmouth inlet
	- **d.** Variable geometry inlet
4. The compressor pressure ratio is defined as:
	- **a.** Inlet pressure ÷ Outlet pressure
	- **b.** Outlet pressure ÷ Inlet pressure
	- **c.** Turbine pressure ÷ Compressor pressure
	- **d.** Air velocity ÷ Pressure energy
5. What is the primary function of the compressor section?
	- **a.** Convert fuel into energy
	- **b.** Supply compressed air for combustion
	- **c.** Cool the turbine blades
	- **d.** Direct exhaust gases rearward
6. Which of the following is an advantage of a centrifugal flow compressor?
	- **a.** Large frontal area
	- **b.** High manufacturing cost
	- **c.** Low weight and simplicity in design
	- **d.** Requires high starting power
7. In an axial flow compressor, each pair of rotor and stator vanes is called a:
	- **a.** Stage
	- **b.** Section
	- **c.** Spool
	- **d.** Passage
8. The combustion chamber mixes and burns the air-fuel mixture. What percentage of air is approximately used as primary air for combustion?
	- **a.** 10–20%
	- **b.** 25–35%
	- **c.** 45–55%
	- **d.** 65–75%
9. Which type of turbine blade produces turning force mainly by aerodynamic action and pressure drop across the blades?
	- **a.** Impulse blade
	- **b.** Reaction blade
	- **c.** Impulse-reaction blade
	- **d.** Shrouded blade
10. The accessory section of a gas turbine engine provides power for:
	 - **a.** Thrust production only
	 - **b.** Fuel pumps, generators, and hydraulic systems
	 - **c.** Compressor cooling only
	 - **d.** Turbine speed regulation
