Here is the list of components and their descriptions:

List of Components:
1. Air Inlet (Light Blue): The air inlet is the first rotor in the compressor. Its primary task is to accelerate a large mass flow of air in the bypass flow and thus provide the main thrust.
2. Compressor (Green): The task of the compressors is to ingest air and compress it before it is fed into the combustor. The low-pressure compressor is responsible for precompressing the air, while the high-pressure compressor handles main compression. What sets an advanced turbofan engine apart is that the blades and disk are manufactured as a single part—known as a “blisk.”
3. Combustor (Gold): Inside the combustor, the compressed air flowing into the chamber is mixed with fuel, where it burns at a temperature of about 1,700 degrees Celsius.
4. Turbine (Metallic Grey): In a turbine, the energy contained in the gas flow emerging at high pressure and temperature from the combustor is converted into mechanical energy. The high-pressure turbine, comprising multiple blades, drives the high-pressure compressor. The low-pressure turbine drives the low-pressure compressor and the fan, which in turn generates the bulk of the thrust. The GTF’s low-pressure turbine rotates much faster than that of a conventional turbofan engine.
5. Exhaust (Dark Blue): The exhaust nozzle accelerates the exhaust gases exiting the engine, converting thermal energy into kinetic energy to produce thrust. This component is crucial for maximizing engine efficiency and performance.
