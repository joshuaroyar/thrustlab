1. Upon clicking the assembly feature, the components are automatically arranged along the side in a fixed top-to-bottom order. Every time the assembly is reset, the components return to the same arrangement

Fix: Modify the assembly behavior so that the components are randomly shuffled upon the first activation of the assembly feature and after every reset.

2. When an incorrect arrangement is attempted for the combustion, compression, and turbine components—while the intake and 
exhaust components are placed in their correct locations—the resulting score is only 20/100. The current scoring system appears to disregard the correct placement of other components once an error is made in the arrangement sequence of an earlier component. As a result, partial correctness is not adequately reflected in the final score.

Fix: Revise the scoring and evaluation logic to ensure that points are awarded based on the correctness of each individual component’s placement rather than being heavily penalized by a single early mistake.

3. Based on the reference image, the “Assembly & Disassembly Activity” text under the Activity Module card is misaligned and pushed too far toward the left edge. The label appears partially compressed within the card, affecting readability and overall visual balance. This positioning makes the text look cut off.

Fix: Refine the text layout and alignment of the Assembly & Disassembly Activity label to ensure proper spacing and readability.

4. The score is displayed while the user is still in the process of assembling the components. Showing the score during the assembly phase may distract learners and create unnecessary pressure, rather than allowing them to focus on understanding the correct arrangement of components.

Fix: Temporarily hide or disable score display during the assembly process and reveal the score only after the user completes the activity or submits the final arrangement.
Possible approach include:
Displaying the score only after a “Save” action

5. The side components are too small and leave an awkward empty space below.

Fix: Maximize the available space to improve layout balance and usability.

6. The presence of labels of the five basic components during assembly makes the activity easier for students to complete.

Fix: Remove the labels during assembly to further test students’ understanding and performance.

7. When the audio for the Turbofan Engine model is played, both ElevenLabs and fallback tts audio is played.

Fix: Ensure that only ElevenLabs audio source is played, and the fallback tts audio is only used when ElevenLabs encounters an issue.
