# Profile Page

This document outlines the profile page features and functionalities for users of the ThrustLab platform. The profile page allows users to view and manage their personal information, account settings, and preferences.

## Features

- **View Personal Information**: Users can view their name, email address, and other personal details.
- **Edit Profile**: Users can update their personal information, including changing their profile picture.
- **Account Settings**: Users can manage their account settings, such as changing their password and configuring privacy settings.
- **Activity Log**: Users can view their recent activities and interactions on the platform.
- **Delete Account**: Users have the option to delete their account permanently.

## Navigation

The profile page can be accessed from the main navigation menu under the "Account" section. Users can click on their username or profile picture to navigate to their profile page.

## Security

The profile page is secured and requires users to be logged in to access their personal information. Sensitive actions, such as changing passwords or deleting accounts, require additional verification steps to ensure account security.

## Support

For any issues or questions regarding the profile page, users can contact ThrustLab support through the help center or by submitting a support ticket.
